<a id="move"></a>

### ![Movement](assets/icons/movimiento_ico.svg) Movement

Move up to your Movement value. End in a stable position and do not move through enemies.

<a id="jump"></a>

### Jump

Jump up to 2″ at a time, within your Movement allowance. Increase the jump by 1″ per Chakra spent.

![Diagram: Jump](assets/diagrams/jump.svg)

<a id="climb"></a>

### Climb

While touching terrain, spend Movement equal to the height from the ninja’s head to the edge (rounded up), plus 1 Chakra per climb. Descending costs no Movement.

![Diagram: Climb](assets/diagrams/en/climb.svg)

<a id="charge"></a>

### Charge

If B was in line of sight (LoS) at the start and A ends within Engagement Range (ER), perform a free Attack or Jutsu (one charge per activation). A charge attack applies −1 to the defender’s Skill. If A wins, it may use its remaining Movement. Forced movement does not trigger a charge.

![Diagram: Charge](assets/diagrams/en/charge.svg)

<a id="follow-up"></a>

### Attack and Finish

After winning a charge attack, A may spend its remaining Movement to reach B within ER again and attack with half its Attack dice, with no Defense roll or charge modifier. Once only, against the original target.

![Diagram: Attack and Finish](assets/diagrams/en/follow-up.svg)

<a id="impulse"></a>

### Burst

Move 2″ in any direction. Apply the Movement rules, including a charge if one has not already been made.

<a id="use-item"></a>

### ![Use Item](assets/icons/equipo_ico.svg) Use Item

Use an equipped weapon or item and resolve its effect.

<a id="jutsu"></a>

### ![Jutsu](assets/icons/escuela_jutsu.svg) Jutsu

Spend the indicated Chakra. Depending on the technique, use it as an action, during an Attack, Charge or Mokushin. By default, the target does not roll Defense.

<a id="mokushin"></a>

### ![Mokushin](assets/icons/mokushin_ico.svg) Mokushin

Spend 1 action during your activation to remain alert until your next activation or until you react. If you have not yet activated this round, you may spend 1 action in advance when targeted by an attack or Jutsu. Ignore the −1 Skill modifier from a charge. You may cancel a Jutsu by spending the same amount of Chakra as the attacker, or use defensive Jutsus or a smoke bomb. Mark the state when it is used.

<a id="recover-chakra"></a>

### ![Recover Chakra](assets/icons/chakra_ico.svg) Recover Chakra

Recover 1 Chakra per action spent (1–3 actions).
