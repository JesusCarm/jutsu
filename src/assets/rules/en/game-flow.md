### Rounds and Activations

The mission determines the number of rounds. The player with fewer units standing gains initiative; on a tie, the highest roll on 1D6 wins. In the first round, deploy units alternately, starting with the player who has initiative. That player chooses who activates first. Players alternate activating one unit that has not yet activated. Each activation allows up to 3 different actions. The round ends when all units have activated.
