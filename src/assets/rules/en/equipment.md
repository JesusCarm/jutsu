<a id="weapons"></a>

### ![Weapons](assets/icons/armas_ico.svg) Weapons

Thrown, single-use weapons. Use the Use Item action unless stated otherwise.

<a id="kunai"></a>

### ![Kunai](assets/icons/kunai.svg) Kunai

**Cost:** 1 action.

A ranged attack with 2D6.

<a id="shuriken"></a>

### ![Shuriken](assets/icons/shuriken.svg) Shuriken

**Cost:** 1 action.

A ranged attack with 3D6.

<a id="explosive-tag"></a>

### ![Explosive Seal](assets/icons/sello_explosivo.svg) Explosive Seal

**Cost:** 2 actions.

Attach to a Kunai: +3D6 and 1″ of forced retreat per hit. Costs 2 actions in the same activation (Kunai and Seal).

<a id="smoke-bomb"></a>

### ![Smoke Bomb](assets/icons/bomba_humo.svg) Smoke Bomb

**Cost:** 1 action.

Evade an attack or Jutsu and move up to your full Movement value. This is not a Movement action and does not trigger a Charge.

<a id="soldier-pill"></a>

### ![Soldier Pill](assets/icons/pildora_soldado.svg) Soldier Pill

**Cost:** 1 action.

For the current round: +2″ Movement, +2D6 Attack and +2D6 Defense.

<a id="curry-pill"></a>

### ![Curry Pill](assets/icons/pildora_curry.svg) Curry Pill

**Cost:** 1 action.

Recover 4 Health, up to the maximum.

<a id="cake-pill"></a>

### ![Cake Pill](assets/icons/pildora_pastel.svg) Cake Pill

**Cost:** 1 action.

Recover 3 Chakra, up to the maximum.
