### ![Attack](assets/icons/ataque_ico.svg) Attack

A and B must be within ER. A rolls a number of D6 equal to its Attack; B rolls a number of D6 equal to its Defense. Each die that equals or exceeds the opponent’s Skill is a success. B chooses to use all successes to **block** (cancel hits) or **trade blows** (inflict hits). Each hit removes 1 Health. The unit inflicting more hits wins; the unit receiving more hits moves back one inch per hit received. On a tie or if neither unit receives hits, both move back 2″. Obstacles during this retreat do not cause collisions.

![Diagram: Attack](assets/diagrams/en/attack.svg)

<a id="ranged"></a>

### Ranged Attack

The target must be in LoS and must not have at least ⅓ of its body in cover. The target does not roll Defense.

<a id="rear"></a>

### Rear Attack

If the attacker is outside the defender’s LoS, the defender does not roll Defense. No other modifiers apply.

<a id="collision"></a>

### Collisions

If terrain stops a unit’s forced retreat from hits, it suffers 1 damage per inch of movement remaining (x″ = −x Health).

![Diagram: Collisions](assets/diagrams/en/collision.svg)

<a id="fall"></a>

### Falls

An involuntary fall causes 1 hit per 2″ of height.

![Diagram: Falls](assets/diagrams/en/fall.svg)
