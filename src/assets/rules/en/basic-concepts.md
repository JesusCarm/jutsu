### Engagement Range (ER)

A and B are within Engagement Range (ER), also called Interaction Distance (ID), if they are 1″ or less apart.

![Diagram: Engagement Range (ER)](assets/diagrams/di.svg)

<a id="line-of-sight"></a>

### Line of Sight (LoS)

A 180° frontal arc. A can see B, but not C.

![Diagram: Line of Sight (LoS)](assets/diagrams/ldv.svg)

<a id="cover"></a>

### Cover

From the observer’s eye level, a unit with at least ⅔ of its body hidden by terrain or other units is outside LoS. For ranged attacks, at least ⅓ is sufficient.

![Diagram: Cover](assets/diagrams/cover.svg)

<a id="rounding"></a>

### Rounding

Round down unless stated otherwise.
