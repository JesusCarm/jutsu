## Hidden Techniques

<a id="jutsu-bola-de-energia"></a>

### Energy Ball

**Chakra:** 3 · **Timing:** Attack · **Range:** ER.

Make a 3D6 attack. If at least 1 hit is scored, move the target 3″ in a direction chosen by the attacker. This is not Movement and does not trigger a Charge.

<a id="jutsu-clon"></a>

### Clone

**Chakra:** 3 · **Timing:** Mokushin · **Range:** Self.

Evade an attack or Jutsu and move up to 4″. This is not Movement and does not trigger a Charge.

<a id="jutsu-multiplicacion-de-cuerpos"></a>

### Body Multiplication

**Chakra:** 5 · **Timing:** Attack · **Range:** ER.

Make two consecutive, separate attacks against the same target. The target cannot trade blows.
