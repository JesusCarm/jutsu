## Taijutsu

<a id="jutsu-puerta-de-la-velocidad"></a>

### Gate of Speed

**Chakra:** 1–3 · **Timing:** Movement · **Range:** Self.

Gain 1″ of movement per Chakra spent, up to 3″.

<a id="jutsu-puerta-de-la-fuerza"></a>

### Gate of Strength

**Chakra:** 1–3 · **Timing:** Attack · **Range:** Self.

Gain 1D6 Attack per Chakra spent, up to 3D6.

<a id="jutsu-puerta-protectora"></a>

### Protective Gate

**Chakra:** 1 per success · **Timing:** Mokushin · **Range:** Self.

Cancel 1 attacker success per Chakra spent, up to 5.
