## Medical Techniques

<a id="jutsu-curacion"></a>

### Healing

**Chakra:** 3 · **Timing:** Action · **Range:** LoS.

An allied unit recovers 3 Health or 3 Chakra, up to its maximum.

<a id="jutsu-cambiazo"></a>

### Substitution

**Chakra:** 3 · **Timing:** Mokushin · **Range:** Self.

Evade an attack and move up to 4″ in any direction.

<a id="jutsu-cirugia"></a>

### Surgery

**Chakra:** 5 · **Timing:** Attack · **Range:** ER.

+1D6 Attack. Each hit removes 1 Chakra and reduces the target’s maximum Chakra by 1. The reduction is cumulative for the rest of the game.
