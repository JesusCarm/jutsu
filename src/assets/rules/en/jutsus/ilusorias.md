## Illusion Techniques

<a id="jutsu-ojo-rojo"></a>

### Red Eye

**Chakra:** 3 · **Timing:** Reaction to a Jutsu · **Range:** Self.

Spend 1 available action to copy the incoming Jutsu. Use it once during the next activation at half its Chakra cost (rounded down).

<a id="jutsu-pupila-roja"></a>

### Red Pupil

**Chakra:** 3 · **Timing:** Mokushin · **Range:** Self.

Evade an attack and move up to 4″. This is not Movement and does not trigger a Charge.

<a id="jutsu-intercambio"></a>

### Swap

**Chakra:** 5 · **Timing:** Action/Mokushin · **Range:** LoS, up to 8″.

Swap positions with any unit in range. This is not Movement and does not trigger a Charge.
