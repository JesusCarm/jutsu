## Gentle Defense

<a id="jutsu-torbellino-de-la-villa"></a>

### Village Whirlwind

**Chakra:** 3 · **Timing:** Mokushin · **Range:** Self.

Resolve the attack normally. Half the hits received (rounded down) are applied to the attacker instead of the defender.

<a id="jutsu-palma-de-vacio"></a>

### Void Palm

**Chakra:** 3 · **Timing:** Action/Mokushin · **Range:** ER.

Push the enemy 4″ in the opposite direction. During Mokushin, evade the attack. This is not Movement and does not trigger a Charge.

<a id="jutsu-anti-chakra"></a>

### Anti-Chakra

**Chakra:** 5 · **Timing:** Mokushin · **Range:** Self.

When targeted by a Jutsu, the attacker must pay twice its original cost or the Jutsu is canceled and the action is spent.
