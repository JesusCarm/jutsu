## Animal Spirit

<a id="jutsu-potencia-animal"></a>

### Animal Power

**Chakra:** 3 · **Timing:** Action · **Range:** Self.

Choose Movement, Attack or Defense. Increase its current value by 50% (rounded down) for its next use.

<a id="jutsu-cambiapieles"></a>

### Skin Shedding

**Chakra:** 3 · **Timing:** Mokushin · **Range:** Self.

Evade an attack or Jutsu and move up to 4″. This is not Movement and does not trigger a Charge.

<a id="jutsu-cuatro-garras"></a>

### Four Claws

**Chakra:** 5 · **Timing:** Action · **Range:** Variable.

Make a 6D6 attack against an enemy in range. If the enemy is eliminated or outside ER, leap to another enemy within 8″ and attack with 4D6. If possible again, leap to a third enemy within 6″ and attack with 2D6. If there is no new target, move 6″ and end the technique. This is not Movement and does not trigger a Charge.
