## Fire Techniques

<a id="jutsu-bola-de-fuego"></a>

### Fireball

**Chakra:** 3 · **Timing:** Action · **Range:** Up to 8″.

A ranged attack with 4D6.

<a id="jutsu-llamarada"></a>

### Flare

**Chakra:** 3 · **Timing:** Mokushin · **Range:** Self.

Cancel the attack and move up to 4″. This is not Movement and does not trigger a Charge.

<a id="jutsu-fenix"></a>

### Phoenix

**Chakra:** 5 · **Timing:** Action · **Range:** 8″ path.

Make a separate 4D6 attack against every unit along the path, friendly or enemy. Terrain and miniatures do not block the path.
