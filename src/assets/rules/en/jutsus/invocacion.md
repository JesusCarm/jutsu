## Weapon Summoning

<a id="jutsu-lluvia-de-acero"></a>

### Steel Rain

**Chakra:** 3 · **Timing:** Action · **Range:** LoS.

Summon and throw two single-use Kunai at a visible enemy, following the Kunai rules.

<a id="jutsu-arsenal"></a>

### Arsenal

**Chakra:** 3 · **Timing:** Action · **Range:** Self.

Summon a thrown weapon or smoke bomb and use it immediately for free. It does not count towards the equipment limit.

<a id="jutsu-fuegos-artificiales"></a>

### Fireworks

**Chakra:** 5 · **Timing:** Action · **Range:** LoS, up to 10″.

Fire two rockets, each resolving a separate 4D6 attack against the same target or different targets.
