## Gentle Techniques

<a id="jutsu-ojo-blanco"></a>

### White Eye

**Chakra:** 3 + 1/round · **Timing:** Action · **Range:** Self.

LoS becomes 360°. Ignore charge and rear attack modifiers. Spend 1 Chakra at the start of each round to maintain the technique, or it ends.

<a id="jutsu-anulacion"></a>

### Nullification

**Chakra:** 3 · **Timing:** Mokushin · **Range:** Self.

Completely cancel an attack directly targeting this ninja.

<a id="jutsu-puntos-de-presion"></a>

### Pressure Points

**Chakra:** 5 · **Timing:** Action · **Range:** ER.

The target cannot spend Chakra until the end of its next activation.
