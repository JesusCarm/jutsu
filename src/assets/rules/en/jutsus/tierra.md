## Earth Techniques

<a id="jutsu-arenas-movedizas"></a>

### Quicksand

**Chakra:** 3 · **Timing:** Action · **Range:** LoS.

The enemy is immobilized until the end of its next activation. At the start of that activation, it may spend an action and pass a 1D6 roll against its own Skill to break free. Failure costs the action.

<a id="jutsu-manto-de-arena"></a>

### Sand Cloak

**Chakra:** 3 · **Timing:** Mokushin · **Range:** Self.

Absorb up to 4 hits without damage or displacement. The technique ends once all 4 hits have been absorbed.

<a id="jutsu-tumba-del-desierto"></a>

### Desert Tomb

**Chakra:** 5 · **Timing:** Action · **Range:** LoS, up to 8″.

Make a 5D6 attack, then immobilise the target until its next activation. To break free at the start of that activation, pass a 1D6 roll against the user’s Skill. While trapped, the target cannot move, attack, or be targeted by attacks or Jutsus.
