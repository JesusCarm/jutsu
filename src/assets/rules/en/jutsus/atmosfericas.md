## Atmospheric Techniques

<a id="jutsu-rayo"></a>

### Lightning

**Chakra:** 3 · **Timing:** Action · **Range:** Self.

Each hit from the next attack deals 2 damage instead of 1.

<a id="jutsu-viento"></a>

### Wind

**Chakra:** 3 · **Timing:** Mokushin · **Range:** Self.

Evade an attack and move up to 4″. This is not Movement and does not trigger a Charge.

<a id="jutsu-tormenta"></a>

### Storm

**Chakra:** 5 · **Timing:** Action · **Range:** 8″ radius.

Each enemy within the radius receives a separate 3D6 attack; 2 damage per hit.
